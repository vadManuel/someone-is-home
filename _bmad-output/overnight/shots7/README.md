# shots7 — the scan lines on a ground that emits

Eleven screens at 600×1300, at three strengths of the glass, so the owner's report can be looked at
rather than argued about. **Both lit grounds are in here**, because the report came in two parts and
the second one named the worse of them:

- **bone** — `join`, `perms`, `lobby`, `winresidents`: dark ink on a mid-tone ground.
- **amber** — `lock`, `locknotify`, `scan`: black glyphs on a full `Amber.Bright` field.
  `tally`, `wininsiders`, `notify` and `banner` are the mixed case, an amber block on an otherwise
  black panel. The last two are the **Egress banner**, and they were found by the guard rather than
  by reading the palette — see `../2026-08-21.md`.
